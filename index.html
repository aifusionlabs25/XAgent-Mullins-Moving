What Evan is designed to handle

Evan is framed around a real moving customer journey: someone researching a move, trying to understand cost, timing, availability, packing, and next steps before they are ready to speak with the office.

✓
Quote qualificationCollects origin, destination, timing, move size, special items, and preferred contact method.
✓
Approved answers onlyAnswers from Mullins-approved information and escalates uncertain questions instead of guessing.
✓
Email handoff packageSends the customer a thank-you summary, sends Mullins qualified lead details, and sends session notes with timestamps. CRM tooling can be added on request.
